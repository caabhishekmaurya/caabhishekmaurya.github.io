01 / ABOUT

About the firm

Practical insight for
business and compliance.

Maurya & Associates is a sole-proprietorship Chartered Accountancy practice based in Rudrapur, Udham Singh Nagar, Uttarakhand. The practice is owned by CA. Abhishek Kumar Maurya and is registered with the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India under Firm Registration Number 021524C.

The firm’s work encompasses taxation, regulatory and corporate compliance, and professional assignments for businesses and organisations. This website provides general information about the practice and selected technical developments.
