Maurya & Associates is a sole-proprietorship Chartered Accountancy practice based in Rudrapur, Udham Singh Nagar, Uttarakhand.

The practice is owned by CA. Abhishek Kumar Maurya. Its professional work includes taxation, regulatory and corporate compliance for businesses and organisations, including non-profit entities.

The professional background of the proprietor includes work in GST, direct taxation, audit, NGO and society compliance, corporate matters, labour-law compliance and MSME-related financial documentation.
